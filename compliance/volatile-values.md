# Volatile values

Dollar thresholds and rates that the guide relies on, by year. Some change every year with inflation; others are set in law and change only when Congress acts. Use the column for the year the gift was received.

The "Verified" column shows how each row was checked: **P** means checked against the original IRS, statute, or regulation text; **S** means summarized from two or more professional sources (accounting or law firms) and not yet checked against the original. Page numbers refer to the PDF pages of the IRS document, not its printed page numbers.

2026 figures read directly from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 sec. 4.33 (p. 24); 2025 figures from Rev. Proc. 2024-40 sec. 2.34. Both safe harbors apply only when the payment is part of a fundraising campaign and the charity tells the donor how much is deductible (Pub. 1771).

## Donor-side rules from the 2025 tax law (effective tax years beginning after 2025)

These change what donors can deduct, so they affect receipt and appeal language, not your ledger coding.

| Row | Rule | Source | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|
| <a id="nonitemizer"></a>Non-itemizer deduction | Up to $1,000 ($2,000 married filing jointly) of cash gifts to public charities; gifts to donor-advised funds and supporting organizations do not qualify; not indexed | IRC 170(p) as added by Pub. L. 119-21 | S |
| <a id="itemizer-floor"></a>Itemizer floor | Only giving above 0.5% of AGI is deductible. Amounts lost to the floor carry forward only in limited cases (when the donor is also carrying forward gifts above the percentage ceiling); treat them as generally lost | IRC 170 as amended by Pub. L. 119-21 | S |
